Calculation logic:

basic amount = quantity × rate

CGST amount = basic amount × CGST percentage / 100

SGST amount = basic amount × SGST percentage / 100

Total GST = CGST amount + SGST amount

Grand Total = subtotal + total GST - discount

Balance Due = grand total - amount paid

Balance Due must not be negative.

Payment status:

If balance due <= 0:
    Paid

Otherwise:
    Pending
